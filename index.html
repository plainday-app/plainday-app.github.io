plainday

A calm day planner that keeps your day clear.

Type a plain sentence and it finds the day, time and repeat for you. Add your weekly timetable or shifts once and they show up every week. Add tasks with widgets and Siri without opening the app. No account, no ads, no tracking: your data lives only in your own iCloud.
